Blueprint & Cheat-Sheet Ujian Masuk PPDS Orthopaedi

6 Pilar Utama Ujian Tulis, OSCE & Wawancara PPDS

1. Upper Extremity & Hand (25%)

Clavicle, Floating Shoulder, Shoulder/Elbow Dislocation, Proximal/Shaft Humerus, Distal Radius, Scaphoid, Bennett/Rolando, Kanavel, CTS/Cubital Tunnel, AC Joint.

2. Lower Extremity & Pelvis (25%)

Femoral Neck (Garden/MFCA), Open Tibia (Gustilo-Anderson & ΔP < 30), Ankle/Maisonneuve, Pelvic Ring (Young-Burgess & ATLS Binder).

3. Pediatric Orthopaedics (20%)

Supracondylar Humerus (Gartland, AIN, Pink Pulseless), Salter-Harris I–V, Septic Arthritis (Kocher), DDH (Ortolani/Barlow) & CTEV (Ponseti CAVE).

4. Spine & Neuro-Emergency (10%)

Cauda Equina Syndrome (PVR > 200 mL, < 24–48h), ASIA Scale, Neurogenic Shock (hipotensi + bradikardia) vs Spinal Shock (Bulbocavernosus).

5. Tumor & Sports Medicine (10%)

Osteosarcoma (Codman/Sunburst), GCT (Soap-bubble), Osteochondroma, Ewing (t(11;22)), Enneking, ACL/PCL/Meniscus & Unhappy Triad.

6. Basic Science & Infection (10%)

Primary vs Secondary Bone Healing, Perren's Strain Theory, Wolff's Law, RANKL-OPG, Hypertrophic vs Atrophic Nonunion, Cierny-Mader.
