Kisi-Kisi Ujian Tulis, OSCE & Wawancara Masuk PPDS Orthopaedi

1. Upper Extremity Trauma (25%)

Clavicle (Allman/Neer), Floating Shoulder (SSSC), Anterior Shoulder Dislocation (Bankart/Hill-Sachs/Axillary n.), Humeral Shaft (Holstein-Lewis/Radial n.), Distal Radius (Colles/Smith/Barton, Galeazzi/Monteggia).

2. Lower Extremity & Pelvis (25%)

Femoral Neck (Garden/Pauwels, MFCA AVN), Open Tibia (Gustilo-Anderson & Compartment Syndrome ΔP < 30), Ankle (Weber/Lauge-Hansen/Maisonneuve), Pelvic Ring (Young-Burgess & ATLS Binder).

3. Pediatric Orthopaedics (20%)

Supracondylar Humerus (Gartland, AIN & Pink Pulseless Hand), Salter-Harris I–V, Septic Arthritis vs Transient Synovitis (Kocher Criteria), DDH (Ortolani/Barlow) & CTEV (Ponseti CAVE).

4. Spine & Neuro-Emergency (10%)

Cauda Equina Syndrome (Saddle anesthesia, PVR > 200 mL, dekompresi < 24–48 jam), Spinal Cord Injury (ASIA Scale, Neurogenic Shock vs Spinal Shock).

5. Bone Tumor & Sports/Knee (10%)

Osteosarcoma (Codman triangle/Sunburst), Giant Cell Tumor (Soap-bubble epiphyseal), Osteochondroma, Enneking Staging, ACL/PCL/Meniscus & Unhappy Triad O'Donoghue.

6. Basic Science & Infection (10%)

Primary vs Secondary Bone Healing, Wolff's Law, RANKL-OPG, Nonunion (Hypertrophic vs Atrophic), serta Cierny-Mader Osteomyelitis.
